Attention & evidence

Each item counts once in the four queues. A decision you can make comes first, followed by agent work and maintainer action. Other tracked work includes branches waiting for prerequisites, drafts and items without a recorded action. Choose “Show this queue” to see the items in its count.

Individual reasons can overlap. Selecting a reason includes items with that reason in any queue. Review changes, draft reviews and replies have separate filters. Recorded qualification is distinct from approval. Missing live check and review data remain unknown. An unanswered review remains open until there is evidence that it was addressed.

Show history includes delivered, withdrawn, retired and merged work. Dates show when the recorded state was observed or changed.
